# Design candidate acquisition, replacement, and widening

## Question

How do `Find a Site` and `Check a Site` ingest curated, user-supplied, and later licensed candidates without confusing locations, parcels, buildings, listings, availability, or permission? Decide deduplication, identity ambiguity, candidate replacement, geography widening, stopping conditions, and what happens when the lawful candidate pool is exhausted.
