# Prove or defer Concept Studio

## Question

Can one prebuilt warehouse concept be transformed and tested independently of Google tiles, then displayed over live Maps 3D at acceptable desktop performance? Prototype the positive, known-conflict, and ambiguous cases and decide whether `FIT/FUTURE` enters the MVP, appears only as a clearly labeled visual concept, or is deferred entirely.
