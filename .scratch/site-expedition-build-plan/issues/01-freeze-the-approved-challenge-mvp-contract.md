# Freeze the approved challenge MVP contract

## Question

Consolidate the already-approved grilling decisions and feasibility constraints into one concise, traceable implementation contract without asking the user to repeat or reapprove product choices. What is committed for the Warehouse hero and secondary Missions, what is an honest fallback, what is conditional on a bounded proof, and what is explicitly deferred?

## Answer

Frozen in [Challenge MVP implementation contract](../mvp-contract.md). Product vision was not reopened.

**Committed:** US-only desktop single-user Site Expedition; Warehouse / Light Industrial hero for a regional distribution operator; Home, Farm, and Data Center as thinner proof recipes; structured Mission Plan confirmation; curated or user Candidate Sites with `LISTED` / `USER SITE` / `POTENTIAL` labels; Strong Fit / Reject / Conditional with unknowns never silently passing; question-owned Workstreams; selective flood-rewind; deterministic guards around the model; Aerial-then-3D presentation; one cited verification brief.

**Honest fallbacks:** cache/fixture-first Mireye while 56 credits remain; Routes as `UNKNOWN` until a later approved test; no live commercial inventory; FABDEM out of the default path; survivors usually Conditional on route, Market Availability, and capacity.

**Conditional proof:** `FIT` / `FUTURE` only after the independent three-case Concept Test; `environmental-record` only after a real drilldown proof.

**Deferred:** collaboration/SaaS, uploads, unrestricted discovery, licensed nationwide inventory, permit-ready CAD, ten presets, AWS, labor-access, unvalidated land-change, climate ensembles, and any capacity/zoning/title claim without the responsible authority.

Domain language gained **Listed Site** and **User Site** in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md). Later tickets still own budgets, the Texas candidate file, schemas, runtime, and the demo script.
