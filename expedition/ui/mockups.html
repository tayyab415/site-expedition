What was confused

The pile we were carrying

The live product is a cream form that compiles a Mission Plan, then a night board with Scout / Screen / Compare / Sketch, Street / Earth / Orbit / 3D / Map, Keep / Pass, Screen N pins, a homework rail, and a comparison table that eats itself. The globe often renders black. Clicking the ground does nothing.

We then stacked Find vs Check, OSM map search, Texas Triangle as a default region, and Concept Studio as a later beat. Find in Chicago can work (16 POTENTIAL map places). Find in Texas dumps OSM on the 4 curated pins and the Screen button lies. Data center has no Texas catalog pins at all, only Ashburn VA and Quincy WA. That is why a Texas DC look can feel like two mute dots.

The sentence that still holds: give a place, read the surface, click the map for more, stay on one page. Two jobs, Examine and Discover. Everything else is a camera, a chip, or another pin.

  • Placeholders look filled (Chicago, IL and the Austin address).
  • Screen 16 / 20 pins counts OSM cards. Run All screens the catalog.

  • Orbit, Street, FUTURE massing, FEMA veto already exist. They are buried.
  • Mireye is in the packet after Screen. It is not the HUD.

Suggestion

Put Mireye on the pin, not behind Screen

Right now Mireye only shows up after an expedition stream. Discover returns mute POTENTIAL pins. Curated Texas warehouses have replay packets, so San Leon can say FEMA. OSM warehouses and almost every data-center pin do not. You click nothing, you learn nothing, you get two or three dots.

Examine should call Mireye when the pin lands. Not the full workstream novel. A short surface packet plus chips:

  • Identity of the coordinate or resolved address.
  • Hard vetoes first. Mapped SFHA is the one people already understand.
  • A few INFORM fields for the mission. Grid and heat for data halls. Rail and flood for warehouses. Always with source names.
  • Named gaps in plain speech. FIT, sale, deliverable MW. Never claimed.

Click a chip or a painted layer and run /v1/ask for that question at that coordinate. That is the “find out more” path. Live spend shows on the credit mark. Replay cache stays free.

Discover may keep OSM as POTENTIAL map features so Texas is not four research pins. It must not look listed. Clicking a Discover pin is Examine, and Examine is where Mireye happens. If we cannot fetch, the HUD says so. Mute pins are how we lost trust.

Data center in Texas needs either OSM + live Mireye on click, or we stop pretending the catalog covers Texas. The catalog does not.
