# Concept Studio

Use this when the user wants to see a labeled FUTURE massing on a Candidate Site. Warehouse is the visual hero; Farm, Home, and Data Center use the same engine with their own presets.

## In

- Mission-scoped presets (cross-dock, bulk, last-mile, cold, flex, fulfillment, truck terminal, office/warehouse hybrid, packing shed, house massing, data-hall shell).
- Schematic interior program overlay (2D plan and 3D cutaway glTF).
- Conceptual DXF and IFC download, labeled **CONCEPTUAL NOT FOR PERMIT**.

## Out

- Stamped or permit-certified drawings.
- Existing-building interior reconstruction from Google tiles or uploaded as-builts.
- Licensed-parcel FIT. The pad is an assumption. `FIT` stays deferred.

## How to show it

1. Confirm the Mission Plan, screen a site, switch the board to FUTURE.
2. Pick a preset. Toggle Schematic interior. Download DXF/IFC from the Concept Studio rail.
3. Keep yaw as an assumption. Do not treat heading as a civil turning-template.

Default warehouse footprint is **80×40×10 m** (`warehouse-cross-dock` / `cross_dock`), matching `expedition/assets/warehouse.gltf`.

## Relay

Call it a visual concept. Say schematic interiors are a program diagram. Say CAD is for coordination, not stamped, not permit-ready, and not FIT.
